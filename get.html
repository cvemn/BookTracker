Get Dogear

A cozy little shelf for the books you read together.

1
Download the app ⬇ Download for Android
2
Open it and tap Install

Tap the download when it finishes. The first time, your Galaxy asks about installing apps from your browser: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, go back, and tap Install.

3
Join the shelf

Copy your invite, open Dogear, tap Got an invite link from your person? and paste it.

Rather not install anything?

Dogear also works right in your browser. Open it in Chrome or Samsung Internet and tap Install or Add to Home screen.
